Sources and changes

Each calculator names its source under its result. All were checked in Nov 2026.

Guidelines used for interpretation

  • Cardiology: ACC/AHA/ACCP/HRS atrial fibrillation 2023; ESC atrial fibrillation 2024; AHA/ACC chest pain 2021; ESC acute coronary syndromes 2023
  • Pulmonary: ATS/IDSA community-acquired pneumonia 2019 and 2025 update; ACP 2015 and ESC 2019 for pulmonary embolism
  • GI and liver: OPTN liver allocation (MELD 3.0, July 2023); ACG upper GI bleeding 2021; ACG acute pancreatitis 2024
  • Sepsis and ICU: Sepsis-3 (2016); Surviving Sepsis Campaign 2021; SOFA-2 (JAMA, Oct 2025) noted
  • Neurology: AHA/ASA acute ischemic stroke 2026

What changed in Nov 2026

  • Corrected scoring: HEART gave 1 point for a "slightly suspicious" history (it is 0); the NIHSS scored one arm and one leg instead of all four limbs; GRACE used wrong point values and is now the published in-hospital nomogram; age and lab ranges in CHA₂DS₂-VASc, GRACE and Glasgow-Blatchford can no longer be ticked twice
  • CHA₂DS₂-VASc: now also shows CHA₂DS₂-VA (ESC 2024) and bases the recommendation on risk factors other than sex; aspirin is no longer offered for stroke prevention
  • MELD: MELD 3.0, the score used for US allocation since 2023, added alongside MELD-Na; MELD-Na now applies the sodium term only above 11, as defined; dialysis is an option
  • Interpretation updates: Wells PE and DVT give the two-tier result guidelines use, with age-adjusted D-dimer; Glasgow-Blatchford uses the ACG 0–1 discharge threshold; Ranson advises moderate fluids and early feeding; qSOFA carries the Surviving Sepsis caution; SOFA mortality bands fixed; NIHSS reflects the 2026 stroke guideline (tenecteplase, minor non-disabling stroke, dual antiplatelet therapy); TIMI and CURB-65 give the rate for each score

For education and clinical decision support only. Always apply clinical judgment. Not a substitute for specialist consultation.
