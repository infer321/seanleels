About

Who I Am

I'm a medical student at USC Keck School of Medicine applying into ophthalmology. My current work at the USC Roski Eye Institute includes independent TriNetX cohort studies, ultra-widefield retinal imaging and vessel segmentation, gene therapy wet-lab research, and ophthalmic training for long-duration spaceflight.

Earlier research spans general surgery, pulmonary and critical care, transplant hepatology, and emergency medicine, including multicenter studies of cirrhosis and liver transplant outcomes.

I also build clinical tools: Eye Atlas, an anatomy-to-pathology ophthalmology study tool; Before You Page Ophtho, a pre-consult checklist for overnight teams; Rounds, a mobile app for clinical rotations; and references for antibiotic selection and clinical scoring.

Before medical school I spent two years in oncology wet-lab research at the USC Mann School of Pharmacy, and a year as a patient liaison at Garfield Medical Center providing medical interpretation in Cantonese, Mandarin, and English.

Where It Began

Field Researcher
Dmanisi, Georgia · Jun – Aug 2019
  • Participated in active excavation at Dmanisi, one of the most significant Homo erectus fossil sites outside Africa, gaining hands-on exposure to paleoanthropological methods and human evolutionary history.
