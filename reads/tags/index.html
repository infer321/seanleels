★ Landmark Glaucoma RCT BMJ, May 2021 Appraised Nov 2026

TAGS: early trabeculectomy for advanced glaucoma

In newly diagnosed advanced open-angle glaucoma, primary trabeculectomy held pressure about 3 mmHg lower than escalating drops, but quality of life at 2 years did not differ. The trial did not show that lower pressure saved vision within 2 years.

Study snapshot

Design
Pragmatic, multicenter, unmasked randomized trial, 1:1, 27 UK centers, intention-to-treat analysis
Population
Adults with newly diagnosed advanced open-angle glaucoma (Hodapp-Parrish-Anderson visual field criteria) in one or both eyes. Patients unable to have incisional surgery or at high risk of trabeculectomy failure were excluded
N
453 randomized: 227 trabeculectomy, 226 medical management. Randomization was by participant; when both eyes qualified, the less severe eye was the index eye and both eyes got the same treatment
Surgery arm
Mitomycin C-augmented trabeculectomy by fellowship-trained glaucoma surgeons. Drops continued while waiting for surgery
Medical arm
Escalating drops per NICE guidance, then oral carbonic anhydrase inhibitor, then augmented trabeculectomy if pressure stayed uncontrolled. Target guidance was below 15 mmHg, set by the clinician
Primary outcome
Vision-related quality of life (VFQ-25) at 24 months
Funding
NIHR Health Technology Assessment programme; ISRCTN56878850

Effect

Pressure was lower with surgery at every visit, 12.4 against 15.1 mmHg at 24 months. That did not translate into a quality-of-life difference (+1.06 VFQ-25 points; the trial was designed to detect 6) or a visual field difference at 2 years. In the medical arm, 17% (39 of 226) had trabeculectomy within 2 years, and the analysis kept them in the medical arm.

What the safety data say

Overall safety events were similar, and no one had unexplained loss of vision right after surgery. But surgery meant more procedures to fix problems (24 patients against 3), and the 3 patients with an irreversible loss of 10 or more letters were all in the surgery arm. Visual acuity was slightly worse after surgery at 24 months, which the authors attribute to early cataract.

What this was not

A null primary result is not proof the two approaches are equivalent. Two years is short for a disease measured over decades, so the trial cannot say whether the 3 mmHg pressure gap prevents vision loss later. Patients at high risk of trabeculectomy failure were excluded, and surgeons were fellowship-trained, so results may not carry over to every setting.

How far to trust it

Moderate for no quality-of-life difference at 2 years; high for the pressure difference

A well-run randomized trial with low attrition, so I trust the pressure result, which was measured by masked observers. The quality-of-life result rests on an unmasked, patient-reported questionnaire and a 2-year window, so I hold it more loosely.

What I'd change

Follow patients long enough to see visual field progression, and make that the primary outcome alongside quality of life. Report how many trabeculectomies needed laser suture lysis, needling or bleb revision. Include newer minimally invasive and tube options as comparators, and recruit more patients from non-White populations.

Clinical significance takeaway

For newly diagnosed advanced open-angle glaucoma, lower the pressure promptly and discuss early MMC-augmented trabeculectomy with the glaucoma attending, especially when a low target pressure is needed. TAGS supports stronger and more sustained pressure control without more serious safety events, and it also shows the cost: more post-operative procedures. Individualize the target, and weigh surgical risk, patient preference and ability to attend close post-operative follow-up. If surgery is declined or not suitable, escalate drops and operate if pressure stays uncontrolled.

Bottom line

TAGS 2021: for newly diagnosed advanced open-angle glaucoma, primary MMC trabeculectomy lowered pressure more than drops (12.4 vs 15.1 mmHg) without changing quality of life at 2 years; discuss early surgery, otherwise escalate drops and operate if pressure stays uncontrolled.
