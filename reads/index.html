Appraisals

Ophthalmology papers, read critically.

What each study found, how far to trust it, and what it would change in clinic. One paper a month.

I started this to become better informed about ophthalmology: by studying the landmark trials that inform our guidelines, and by keeping up with the newest RCTs that change and further our understanding of disease and treatment. These are reviews of papers I enjoy doing in my spare time.

Appraisals

  1. Nov 2026

    LiGHT: laser vs eye drops as first treatment for glaucoma and ocular hypertension

    ★ Landmark Lancet, 2019 Glaucoma RCT Read with the 6-year follow-up
    High certainty, 3-year result

    I would prefer SLT first for eligible patients who tolerate it, given the long-term personal medical and financial burden of daily drops.

  2. Nov 2026

    Protocol S: ranibizumab vs PRP for proliferative diabetic retinopathy

    ★ Landmark JAMA, 2015 Retina RCT Read with CLARITY and the 5-year follow-up
    High certainty, 2-year result

    Protocol S supports anti-VEGF as an effective alternative, but its ongoing treatment burden makes follow-up reliability central to the decision.

Educational commentary, not medical advice. Errors are mine; corrections welcome.
