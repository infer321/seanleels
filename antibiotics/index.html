Sources and changes

Regimens follow the guidelines below; where a recent guideline changed a recommendation, the regimen names it. All were checked in Nov 2026. Always tailor to your local antibiogram.

Guidelines

  • Pneumonia: ATS/IDSA community-acquired pneumonia 2019 and 2025 update; IDSA/ATS hospital- and ventilator-associated pneumonia 2016; GOLD 2025 (COPD exacerbations); CDC influenza antiviral guidance
  • Urinary: IDSA complicated UTI 2025; IDSA/ESCMID uncomplicated cystitis 2010
  • GI and liver: IDSA/SHEA C. difficile 2021 focused update; AASLD ascites and SBP 2021; Tokyo Guidelines 2018; SIS/IDSA intra-abdominal infection; IDSA infectious diarrhea 2017
  • Skin, bone and joint: IDSA skin and soft tissue 2014; IWGDF/IDSA diabetic foot 2023
  • Endocarditis: AHA infective endocarditis 2015; ESC endocarditis 2023; Duke-ISCVID criteria 2023
  • CNS: IDSA bacterial meningitis 2004; IDSA encephalitis 2008; CDC meningococcal chemoprophylaxis 2024
  • STI: CDC STI treatment guidelines 2021; FDA approvals of zoliflodacin and gepotidacin, Dec 2025
  • Systemic: Surviving Sepsis Campaign 2021; IDSA febrile neutropenia 2010 and ASCO/IDSA 2018; IDSA candidiasis 2016; AAO Candida endophthalmitis screening 2022; ATS/CDC/IDSA tuberculosis 2016, CDC 4-month regimen 2022, WHO drug-resistant TB 2022
  • Across infections: ASHP/IDSA/PIDS/SIDP vancomycin AUC dosing 2020; AAAAI/ACAAI drug allergy practice parameter 2022

What changed in Nov 2026

  • CAP: outpatient amoxicillin corrected to 1 g three times daily; duration 3–5 days once stable for non-severe CAP and at least 5 days for severe CAP; corticosteroids suggested for severe CAP only; MRSA coverage limited to the guideline's risk factors; severe-allergy ICU regimen no longer pairs two QT-prolonging drugs
  • Complicated UTI: 7 days for most patients, 5–7 days with a fluoroquinolone, carbapenems reserved for sepsis or known ESBL
  • C. difficile: fidaxomicin listed first for an initial episode; bezlotoxumab removed (discontinued Jan 2025); FDA-approved microbiome products added
  • Endocarditis: gentamicin removed from native-valve MSSA; ampicillin plus ceftriaxone for E. faecalis; empiric ICU regimen changed to vancomycin plus ceftriaxone; Duke-ISCVID criteria and oral step-down added
  • Diabetic foot: osteomyelitis durations updated (6 weeks without resection, about 3 weeks after resection)
  • Other: vancomycin dosing made AUC-guided everywhere; SBP prophylaxis no longer names norfloxacin; empiric fluconazole removed from ICU intra-abdominal infection; meningitis allergy alternative and chemoprophylaxis updated; HSV encephalitis dose corrected and oral step-down removed; CDC inpatient PID regimen added; candidemia eye-exam guidance; new TB regimens; new gonorrhea drugs noted
  • Risk factors: penicillin allergy now explains cephalosporin cross-reactivity, and the other five risk factors add notes; before, they were selectable but had no effect

For education and clinical decision support only. Check your local antibiogram and apply clinical judgment. Not a substitute for infectious diseases consultation.
